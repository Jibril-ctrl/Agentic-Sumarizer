### Here’s the summary of https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_trust_architecture

1. Zero Trust Architecture is a design strategy for IT systems that assumes users and devices should not be trusted by default, even if they are connected to a privileged network.
2. The approach involves establishing identity verification, validating device compliance, and ensuring least privilege access to only explicitly-authorized resources.
3. Traditional approaches to security, such as trusting users and devices within a corporate perimeter or via VPN connections, are insufficient in complex corporate networks.
4. Zero Trust Architecture advocates for mutual authentication, including checking user and device identities without respect to location, and providing access based on confidence of identity and device status.
5. The principles of Zero Trust can be applied to data access and management, using Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) to determine if access can be granted based on attributes of the data, user, and environment.
